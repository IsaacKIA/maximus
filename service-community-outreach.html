The Community Challenge

High Blood Pressure and Diabetes Do Not Give Advance Warning

Too many dedicated church members, elders, and community mothers collapse suddenly from preventable strokes or diabetic emergencies simply because they had not checked their blood pressure in years.

✕ The Silent Crisis in Our Pews

  • ✕ Fear of hospital visits: Members avoid clinics due to fear of high bills, queues, or bad news.
  • ✕ The 'I feel fine' myth: High blood pressure is asymptomatic until it causes heart failure or stroke.
  • ✕ Expired NHIS cards: Many congregation members have lapsed insurance and cannot afford medication.
  • ✕ Unreliable herbal alternatives: Dangerous substitution of verified pharmaceuticals with unregulated concoctions.

✓ The Maximus 'Know Your Numbers' Outreach

  • ✓ Welcoming, dignity-first screening: Set up directly on church grounds in a loving, respectful environment.
  • ✓ Clear number cards: Every member receives their personal numbers explained in simple Ga, Twi, or English.
  • ✓ NHIS guidance & renewal: On-site assistance navigating NHIS mobile renewal and community exemptions.
  • ✓ Ongoing medication follow-up: Immediate access to verified medications and chronic care delivery for those in need.
Clinical Screening Protocol

What We Screen at Every Community Event

Our mobile clinical kits provide rapid, accurate, and hygienic point-of-care testing.

🩺

Blood Pressure & Pulse

Automated clinical-grade sphygmomanometers detecting stage 1, stage 2, and emergency hypertensive crises on the spot.

🩸

Random Blood Glucose

Single-use sterile lancets and instant digital glucometers identifying undiagnosed diabetes or pre-diabetes.

⚖️

BMI & Body Composition

Weight, height, and body mass index classification with personalized dietary advice tailored to Ghanaian home cooking.

👁️

Basic Eye Screening

Visual chart reading to identify cataracts, glaucoma signs, and reading difficulty among church seniors.

🎤

Pulpit Health Talk

Our lead clinician can deliver a 5-to-10 minute presentation during announcements on cardiovascular health and healthy living.

🏥

Clinical Linkage & Counseling

One-on-one confidential consultations where members ask private questions and receive immediate referral notes if required.
