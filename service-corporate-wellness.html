Why Traditional Insurance Isn't Enough

Health Insurance Pays When Staff Are Sick. Wellness Keeps Them Well.

Most Ghanaian employers spend significant sums on private health insurance, yet suffer high sick leave rates because insurance only acts after a health collapse has occurred.

✕ The Unchecked Workforce Cost

  • ✕ Undetected chronic illness: Up to 40% of Ghanaian adults with hypertension do not know they have it until a stroke occurs.
  • ✕ Productivity drain: Employees leaving work repeatedly to queue at clinics for basic medication refills.
  • ✕ Spiraling insurance premiums: Escalating claims year after year because preventive screening was never performed.
  • ✕ Zero visibility for HR: HR Directors receive invoices from HMOs with no actionable insight into workforce health risks.

✓ The Maximus Corporate Partnership

  • ✓ Early risk detection: Rapid, on-site diagnostics that catch hypertension and pre-diabetes before complications develop.
  • ✓ Direct desk delivery for staff: Chronic care subscriptions deliver verified medication to your office so staff never leave work.
  • ✓ Executive Board Report: Anonymised, data-driven report providing aggregate health risks, lifestyle trends, and recommendations.
  • ✓ High employee morale: Staff feel genuinely valued, improving talent retention and corporate loyalty.
