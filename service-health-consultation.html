The Patient Experience

Why Navigating Healthcare in Ghana Is Overwhelming

Too many patients spend hours waiting in public or private hospitals just for a rushed 3-minute chat with a doctor who speaks in Latin medical terms.

✕ The Traditional Clinic Experience

  • ✕ Exhausting hospital queues: Taking an entire day off work, waiting from 7:00 AM just for a routine checkup.
  • ✕ Confusing lab results: Handed a printed lab sheet with asterisks and high numbers, but nobody takes the time to explain them.
  • ✕ Unnecessary expenses: Sent on expensive, repetitive diagnostic wild goose chases before getting a straight answer.
  • ✕ Fragmented advice: No continuity of care. You see a different doctor on every visit and start your story over.

✓ The Maximus Consultation Model

  • ✓ Direct access & prompt appointments: Speak to a qualified clinician at your appointed time without the waiting room fatigue.
  • ✓ Plain English explanations: We explain your diagnosis, what your lab numbers mean, and how your lifestyle affects them.
  • ✓ Targeted specialist referrals: When you truly need a cardiologist, endocrinologist, or surgeon, we connect you directly.
  • ✓ Hybrid consultation options: Choose a relaxed face-to-face consultation in East Legon or a secure WhatsApp session from anywhere.
Clinical Advisory Areas

How We Can Assist You

Our multidisciplinary team provides personalized healthcare guidance across all stages of life.

📊

Lab & Diagnostic Report Review

Did you receive blood test results, lipid profiles, or HbA1c numbers? We interpret each parameter and outline a step-by-step action plan.

🔄

Chronic Disease Management

Structured guidance for managing hypertension, diabetes, and asthma. We review medication effectiveness, diet, and lifestyle habits.

🏛️

NHIS Advisory & Navigation

Assistance understanding what the National Health Insurance Scheme covers, how to renew your card, and how to access approved care.

💊

Medication Review & Second Opinions

Are your pills causing unpleasant side effects? We review all your prescriptions to prevent drug-drug interactions and optimize dosage.

🏥

Specialist & Diagnostic Referral

Need specialized surgery, imaging (MRI/CT), or specialist attention? We connect you to trusted hospitals, clinics, and laboratories in Accra.

🏃

Preventive Health Planning

Personalized wellness plans tailored to your age, family medical history, and occupation to identify cardiovascular risks before they escalate.
