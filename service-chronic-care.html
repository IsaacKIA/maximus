The Chronic Care Reality in Ghana

Why Gaps in Medication Adherence Are Dangerous

In Ghana, non-communicable diseases (NCDs) like hypertension and diabetes are the leading causes of preventable hospitalisations. Most flare-ups happen simply because a patient ran out of medication and delayed refilling for a few days.

✕ The Reactive Refill Cycle

  • ✕ The empty strip surprise: Discovering on a Sunday night that your last blood pressure tablet is gone.
  • ✕ Skipping doses: "I'll buy it when I get paid next week" — leading to silent cardiovascular spikes.
  • ✕ Caregiver burnout: Adult children constantly stressing over whether their elderly parents in Accra took their pills.
  • ✕ Inconsistent brands: Buying whatever generic the corner shop happens to have, causing side effects or dosage errors.

✓ The Maximus Care Subscription

  • ✓ Clockwork automated delivery: Your medication arrives at your door every 28 days without you having to reorder.
  • ✓ Consistent manufacturer batches: Strict continuity of the exact pharmaceutical brand your physician prescribed.
  • ✓ Caregiver peace of mind: We send direct delivery confirmations and adherence updates to family members locally or abroad.
  • ✓ Direct pharmacist access: Monthly adherence reviews, side-effect management, and annual wellness tracking.
